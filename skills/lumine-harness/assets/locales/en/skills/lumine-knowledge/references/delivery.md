# Adoption delivery and acceptance

Use this guidance for knowledge delivery during adoption and upgrades. Tool installation is not content completion. Reuse existing Validation, coverage, task knowledge, and Plan recovery without another state machine or changing migration complete's installation meaning.

## Initial delivery defaults

- A project-specific Lumine collaboration guide: stating goals, the four capabilities, reviewing deliverables, confirming handoffs, resuming sessions, and judging evidence. Link installed stage rules rather than copying another authorization state machine.
- An operation/service guide: available natural-language actions, real entry points, and prerequisites. Distinguish business services from the reader; explain start/restart/stop/status and next steps after failure. Reuse or generate [service extensions](services.md), omitting irrelevant services explicitly.
- Artifact roles and navigation: minimal mandatory AGENTS rules link directly to authorized standards and Wiki; architecture maps belong in Wiki. Distinguish standards, examples, implementation, and confirmed domain language/lasting decisions by authority and implementation status. Product goals and technical change remain in Spec/Plan.
- A material-destination audit: existing rules, architecture, examples, business memory, references, history, personal, and private material each have a destination. Protect originals, integrate unique content, and repair active references before retirement. Preserve and explain anything unsafe to retire; never delete to match a layout.
- Major-capability coverage: answer mechanism, failure, impact, and verification questions rather than counting pages. Preserve complete terms, overloaded terms by context, key invariants, and necessary ADRs in relevant topics, linked to valid decision sources. Do not add domain/decisions directories.

The Agent writes guides from current rules, installed capabilities, and verified entry points. Add them to the knowledge map and link from the home page. New prose and topic filenames follow locale with independent stable identities; do not bulk-rename history. Explain existing capabilities and confirmed language without claiming plans are implemented.

## Layered acceptance

1. **Installation:** distribution, root resolution, configuration, recovery records, and selected-host installation evidence under the migration contract.
2. **Content:** applicable deliverables are navigable, roles and authority agree, and major questions have answers or explicit gaps. Independent reviewers check body versions, sources, and cross-module paths; author checks are not independent review.
3. **Actual usage:** a cold-start Agent independently finds rules, explains a real mechanism, and locates sources. Where available, check human reader navigation, search, and diagrams. Verify project services, real Skill reads, and Hooks separately. Missing capability or environment means unverified, never invented success.

Record this request's checklist, dispositions, evidence, and limitations using docs/templates/adoption-validation.md. Coverage maintains questions, document references, and planned/partial/covered/deferred status. Task knowledge records versioned sources, verified scope, created/revised/retained/deferred decisions, reasons, and content hashes. Neither covered nor wiki check proves semantic accuracy, operation, or user acceptance; the Agent must inspect actual content.

If users narrow or defer work, preserve applicable confirmation, remaining questions, and recovery locations, and state the request's delivery boundary. Upgrades address only affected guides, rules, modules, terms, and gaps while preserving human content and concurrent tasks; do not regenerate the whole library or redefine unrelated business requirements.
