# Design decisions and approval scope

When an unresolved new visual/interaction choice materially affects the goal, provide concrete material for a decision. Reuse existing references, agreed behavior, and approved approaches without seeking approval again for renames, formatting, or entering implementation.

Record the related Spec, material sources, covered pages/states, and actual decision scope. An Agent assessment or approved field does not create approval. Do not present unresolved material choices as approved.

Small styling repairs and reuse of established design need no separate mockup. Complex interactions need sufficient explanatory material, not a mandatory set of HTML, screenshots, component maps, and multiple handoffs. Record implementation deviations in the Plan and verify the real page separately.
